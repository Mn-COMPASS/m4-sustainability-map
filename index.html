Step 2: The FACE lens

Minnesota’s definition of Family and Community Engagement calls it a process and a practice rooted in relational trust that creates equitable partnerships between families, communities, and school staff, with shared responsibility for students’ success. In other words, families and community are partners who carry weight, not an audience to inform or a resource to ask things of.
